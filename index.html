The Problem

Electricity data has no calendar.
Everything else does.

Financial systems have ledgers. Logistics has shipment tracking. Communication has message threads. Electricity — the most consequential commodity in the modern economy — has spreadsheets, siloed SCADA exports, and billing PDFs.

The problem compounds at scale: data centers consuming hundreds of megawatts, industrial facilities managing demand across dozens of rate structures, fusion and advanced nuclear operators modeling power output across multi-decade calendars — all lack a coherent temporal record of electricity that connects operational data to financial outcomes.

Platform Capabilities

What ECAL does

ECAL's capabilities are organized around the calendar — from sub-second IoT ingestion to decade-scale historical modeling.

01

Temporal Normalization

Every data stream — regardless of source frequency, protocol, or timezone — is aligned to a canonical calendar model. Billing periods, rate seasons, and demand windows are first-class objects.

02

Multi-Rate Engine

Any tariff structure across any utility or ISO. Demand charge calculation, TOU pricing, capacity tag assignment, and ratchet clause evaluation — all computed continuously as interval data arrives.

03

Forward Calendar Modeling

Project electricity costs, demand exposure, and grid service revenue across any future billing window. Scenario comparison against historical baselines with statistical confidence intervals.

04

Dispatch Calendar

For BESS and DER assets, ECAL maintains a dispatch calendar — modeling when to charge, discharge, and curtail based on forward rate signals, grid capacity events, and demand forecasts.

05

Carbon Calendar

Marginal emissions intensity by hour, grid region, and generation source — aligned to the consumption calendar so carbon accounting and reporting are continuous, not quarterly estimates.

06

Portfolio Benchmarking

Normalize electricity costs and performance across sites with different utilities, tariffs, and load profiles. Identify outliers, share best practices, and model rate switching scenarios at scale.

07

Regulatory Calendar Layer

Rate case cycles, capacity auction dates, interconnection queue milestones, and demand response enrollment windows — overlaid on the operational calendar so nothing is missed.

08

SCADA-Agnostic Historian

ECAL sits above any existing historian — OSIsoft PI, Ignition, GE Proficy, and others. Data remains in place; ECAL adds the calendar intelligence layer without replacing existing infrastructure.

09

API-First Architecture

Every view, metric, calendar object, and forecast is accessible via REST and GraphQL. Webhooks push calendar events downstream. SDKs in Python, TypeScript, and Go.
